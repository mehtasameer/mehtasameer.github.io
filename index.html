Sameer Mehta

Associate Professor at Rotterdam School of Management, Erasmus University

My research investigates the governance challenges and strategic dynamics that shape modern data markets. I use quantitative methods such as mechanism design, optimization, and cooperative and non-cooperative game theory to study the operational and economic questions that emerge in this domain.

Previously, I was an Assistant Professor at the Gies College of Business, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. I received my Ph.D. in Management Science from The University of Texas at Dallas and my M.Sc. (Integrated) in Mathematics and Scientific Computing from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur.

I currently serve as an Associate Editor at Information Systems Research.
